Luxury Glow Hair & Beauty Salon was founded by Kelly Adams in 2019 in Polokwane.The salon gained popularity throuh word-of-mouth and social media, attracting a loyal client base.

The vision is to become the go to salon for modern and affordable luxury in the community.

Target Audience

Women aged 18-50 seeking high-quality hair and beauty services.
